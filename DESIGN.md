# Visual Design Contract

Scholarship Compass is a guided application planner that helps students turn a
complex profile into a calm, ranked shortlist.

## Product mode

- Mode: `operate`
- Audience and cadence: students and early-career applicants returning during
  scholarship research and application periods.
- Primary workflow: complete a focused profile survey, review a ranked Top 10,
  and continue into contextual advice when needed.

## Visual direction

- Direction name: Clear blue study planner
- Palette family: cobalt and sky blue over cool white and ink neutrals.
- Type treatment: sans-first, friendly but precise, with strong numeric ranking.
- Composition: guided steps first, then a compact comparison list.
- Shape language: soft utility surfaces, crisp blue focus rings, restrained
  corners, and minimal iconography.
- Anti-references: purple AI gradients, sparkle/wand/robot symbols, oversized
  marketing heroes, decorative blobs, glassmorphism, and card grids that hide
  comparison.

## Guardrails

- Preserve semantic tokens and the shared component seams.
- Keep the scholarship workflow on `/discover` and the conversation workspace
  on the existing chat routes.
- Never label deterministic ranking as AI-generated.
- Use no decorative AI iconography; copy communicates the agent capability.
